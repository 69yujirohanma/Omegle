A free alternative to Omegle, Chatroulette and Ome.tv

Looking for an Omegle alternative or an Omegle clone that works in your browser? Randomly lets you talk to strangers with random video chat or text chat, with no account, no download and no tracking. It is open source, runs on GitHub Pages and uses peer-to-peer WebRTC, so your video goes straight to the other person, through a privacy relay by default.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a good Omegle alternative?

Randomly gives you the same core experience as Omegle: random text chat and video chat with strangers, with optional shared interests, no account and no download. It is a small independent project and is not moderated.

Can strangers see my IP address?

By default Randomly forces all video, audio and chat through a TURN relay, so the stranger only sees the relay's address, not yours. Your IP is still visible to the relay and signaling providers, and you can turn this protection off for faster connections.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There are no accounts, emails or passwords. Pick optional interests, choose text or video, and start.

Is it free?

Yes. Randomly is free and open source and runs entirely as a static website on GitHub Pages.

Is it safe for kids?

No. Randomly is for adults aged 18 and over. Chats with strangers are not moderated and can include inappropriate content. Never share personal information.
